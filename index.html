The Person Behind It

Started with one goal: give Auckland genuinely great physio care.

I started Total Body Physio in 2000 — not to build an empire, but because I thought people deserved better than cookie-cutter treatment. Twenty-six years later, I still see patients most days at Titirangi. Staying clinical keeps me sharp and connected to what we're actually here for.

Every physio on our team was chosen not just for their qualifications, but for the kind of person they are. Skilled, caring, and committed to getting you better. We've treated thousands of Aucklanders — from kids with sports injuries to people managing chronic pain — and we're prouder of our reputation than our growth.

"No matter how much the business has grown, I never wanted to lose the hands-on part. That's the whole point."
