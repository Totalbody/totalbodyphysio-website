Other ways to reach us

General enquiries and administration: admin@totalbodyphysio.co.nz

Reschedule or cancel: use the Jenny assistant on our home page (choose "Manage booking"), or ring your clinic. Changes within 24 hours of an appointment incur a $60 late fee, which the clinic may waive for sudden illness or emergencies.

Languages: Mandarin and Cantonese at Pakuranga and Flat Bush, Mandarin at Panmure (Wednesdays), Arabic at Pakuranga and Flat Bush.

Parking: free parking at every clinic.
