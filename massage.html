Massage FAQ

Where can I get a therapeutic or sports massage?

At our Titirangi clinic, 2 Rangiwai Rd, Titirangi Village in West Auckland.

How much is a massage?

60 minutes is $100 and 45 minutes is $80.

Is it a relaxation massage?

No. It's targeted, deep soft-tissue work for people who train hard or have physically demanding jobs, and for recovery between physio sessions.

When is the Titirangi clinic open?

Monday 9am–5pm, Tuesday 7am–5pm, Wednesday and Thursday 9am–5pm, and Friday 7am–5pm. Closed weekends.
